What CPS means

CPS stands for clicks per second. Take the number of clicks you made and divide by the length of the test. Forty clicks in five seconds is 8 CPS. In games where you attack, build or mine by clicking, a higher CPS means more actions in the same amount of time, which is why players test and train it.

Clicking techniques

How you click matters more than how hard you try. These are the common styles, from easiest to most demanding:

TechniqueHow it worksTypical CPS
Normal clickingOne finger presses and releases the button in the usual way.5–8
Two-fingerIndex and middle fingers alternate on the same button.8–12
JitterYou tense your forearm so the finger vibrates quickly on the button.10–14
ButterflyTwo fingers tap the button in quick alternation, like wings.12–20
DragA finger slides across the button so friction makes it register many times.20+

Jitter clicking puts strain on the forearm and wrist, and drag clicking wears out mouse buttons. If you try them, keep the runs short. Some games and servers also treat butterfly and drag clicking as unfair, so check the rules before using them in competition.

Mouse CPS vs spacebar CPS

Most people click a mouse slightly faster than they press a spacebar. A mouse button travels a shorter distance and your index finger is quicker than your thumb. On the other hand, the spacebar is easier to hit with two thumbs in turn. If you compare your score here with your Spacebar Clicker score for the same time mode, you will usually find the mouse ahead by one or two CPS.

Tips for a better CPS score

  • Rest the heel of your hand on the desk and keep the mouse still. Movement wastes energy and can drag the pointer out of the box.
  • Click near the front of the button, where it needs the least force.
  • Warm up with a couple of 5-second runs before trying for a record.
  • If your mouse registers two clicks for one press, it may have a worn switch. That is called double-clicking and it will inflate your score.

Right-clicks are ignored, and the right-click menu is disabled inside the box so it can't interrupt a run.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CPS for a mouse?

Around 6 to 8 CPS is typical with normal clicking. Above 10 is fast, and above 14 usually needs a jitter or butterfly technique.

Does right-clicking count?

No. Only left clicks (or taps on a touch screen) are counted.

Why do my clicks sometimes count double?

Some mice develop a fault where one press registers as two. If your total jumps unexpectedly, your mouse switch may be wearing out.

Is the CPS test the same as the spacebar test?

It uses the same timing and the same result card, but it counts mouse clicks instead of key presses, and it keeps its own personal bests.
