Counter or clicker: what's the difference?

The two names are often used for the same thing, but they answer different questions. A clicker asks how fast you are: it fixes the time and measures the speed. A counter asks how many: it lets you keep going and simply tallies the presses. This page is set up for counting, with an endless mode first and longer timed runs for stamina. If you want a short speed test, the Spacebar Clicker has 1, 5 and 10 second modes.

How the endless counter works

  • The count starts on your first press and the elapsed time runs upward.
  • Press Enter, Esc or the Stop button to end the session and see your result card.
  • Your pace is still measured, so you get an average CPS, your best second and a chart even without a time limit.
  • Each endless session is saved to your recent runs with its length, so you can compare a 40-second session with a 3-minute one.

Challenges to try

A counter is more fun with a target. Here are a few that work well alone or with friends:

ChallengeHow to play
Race to 100Use Endless mode and stop the moment you reach 100 hits. The lowest elapsed time wins.
The minute milePick 60s and aim to stay above 6 CPS for the whole minute. Check the chart to see if any second dropped below.
RelayTwo or three people share one keyboard in Endless mode, swapping every 20 seconds. Add the totals for a team score.
Five-minute marathonPick 300s. This is about pacing and staying relaxed, not speed. Swap thumbs whenever one starts to ache.
Steady handRun 30s and try for a consistency score above 90 percent. It is harder than it sounds.

Looking after your hands in long sessions

Pressing one key hundreds of times in a row is a repetitive movement, and long runs can leave your thumb or wrist sore. Keep your wrist straight and supported, swap hands or thumbs when you feel tension, and take a break of a minute or two between long runs. If you feel pain, numbness or tingling, stop. A score is never worth an injury.

Is the count accurate?

The counter listens for the moment a key goes down and ignores the repeated signals your computer sends when a key is held. That means a sticky or held key cannot inflate the number. On very cheap keyboards a single press can occasionally register twice, which is called chatter. If your count jumps by two on single presses, try another keyboard. Frequently asked questions

How do I stop the endless spacebar counter?

Press Enter, press Esc or click the Stop button. Your result card appears straight away.

Is there a maximum count?

No. Endless mode keeps counting until you stop, and timed runs go up to 600 seconds using the Custom box.

Can two people play on the same device?

Yes. Use Endless mode for a relay, or take turns on the same timed mode and compare the recent runs list.

Do my counts carry over between pages?

No. The Spacebar Counter keeps its own bests and history, separate from the Spacebar Clicker and the CPS Test.
