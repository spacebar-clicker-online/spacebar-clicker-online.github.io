Contact

Contact us

Questions, bug reports and ideas are all welcome. Send them to us through our public feedback board on GitHub.

Feedback board: Open a new message

Click the button, choose New issue, add a short title and describe your question or problem. You need a free GitHub account to post. We usually reply within two to three working days, and you get a notification when we do.

Messages on the feedback board are public, so please do not include personal information such as your phone number or address.

What you can contact us about

  • Bug reports. Something not counting correctly, a button not working, or a layout problem on your device.
  • Feature ideas. New test modes, challenges or settings you would like to see.
  • Privacy requests. Questions about the data described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising concerns. If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, tell us what it showed and roughly when.

Reporting a bug

It helps a lot if you include:

  1. The page and test mode you were using, for example "Spacebar Clicker, 10s".
  2. Your device, browser and keyboard or mouse type.
  3. What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  4. A screenshot, if you can take one.

Before you write

Many common questions are answered in the FAQ sections of the Spacebar Clicker, Spacebar Counter and CPS Test pages.
