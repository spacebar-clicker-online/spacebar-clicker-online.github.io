About Spacebar Clicker

Spacebar Clicker is a small, independent website with free tools for measuring how fast you can press a key or click a mouse.

Why we built it

Spacebar speed tests are popular with gamers, with friends settling a bet, and with anyone who has seen the challenge go round on social media. Many of the testers we tried only gave a total and an average, and some quietly counted a held-down key as dozens of presses. We wanted a tester that counts honestly and tells you something useful about your run.

What makes these tools different

  • Honest counting. Held keys and key-repeat signals are ignored. Only real presses count.
  • More than one number. Every run shows average CPS, your best single second, a consistency score and a per-second chart.
  • Separate records per mode. A 1-second burst never competes with a 60-second endurance run.
  • Works everywhere. Keyboard, mouse and touch screens are all supported, in light or dark mode.
  • Private by design. Scores are stored in your own browser and never uploaded.

How the timing works

The tests run entirely in your browser. When you press a key or click, the page records the time using the browser's high-resolution timer, which is accurate to fractions of a millisecond. The timer starts on your first press, so reaction time is not counted against you. Results are calculated from those timestamps when the run ends.
