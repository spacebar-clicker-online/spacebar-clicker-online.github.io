How to use the spacebar clicker

  1. Choose a time. 10 seconds is the standard test most people compare. Use 1 or 5 seconds for a pure burst, 30 to 100 seconds for stamina, or type any length from 1 to 600 seconds in the Custom box.
  2. Press Space once. The clock does not move until your first press, so you never lose time reaching for the keyboard.
  3. Tap until the bar fills. The hit counter and live CPS update on every press.
  4. Read your result card. It shows your rank, total hits, average CPS, your best single second, how steady you were and a bar chart of every second.
  5. Press Enter to go again. Each time mode keeps its own personal best, so a 1-second burst never competes with a 60-second run.

On a phone or tablet there is no spacebar, so the big on-screen key does the same job. Tap it with one or two thumbs.

Which test length should you pick?

Speed and stamina are different skills. Short tests reward a fast twitch; long ones reward a relaxed hand that can hold a pace. Use the length that matches what you want to measure.

How your score compares

For a 10-second test, most first attempts land between 5 and 7 presses per second. Regular players reach 8 to 10, and anything above 12 held for the full 10 seconds is rare. Expect lower numbers in long tests and higher ones in 1-second bursts. Our guide on what counts as a good spacebar CPS lists ranges for every test length.

Quick tips before your next run

  • Tap, don't push. The key only has to travel a couple of millimetres to register. Lift off as soon as it clicks.
  • Use the side of your thumb and rest your wrist on the desk so the thumb is the only thing moving.
  • Try two thumbs. Alternating left and right thumbs lets each one recover between presses.
  • Start at 90 percent in long tests. An all-out start usually costs more at the end than it gains at the beginning.
  • Practise little and often. A few runs a day improves rhythm faster than one long session that leaves your hand sore.

Fair counting

When you hold a key down, your computer starts sending repeated key signals after a short delay. This tool ignores those repeats, so holding the spacebar adds nothing. Only a full press and release counts as a hit. Timing uses the browser's high-precision clock rather than the regular clock, so the result is accurate to well under a millisecond.

Your scores stay on your device. Personal bests and recent runs are saved in your own browser. Nothing is uploaded, and the Clear button removes them.

Frequently asked questions

What is a spacebar clicker?

A spacebar clicker is a browser test that counts how many times you press the spacebar in a set time and turns that into clicks per second (CPS).

Does holding the spacebar count as clicks?

No. Auto-repeat from a held key is ignored. Only separate presses are counted, so every hit on the board is a real tap.

Why is my score lower in long tests?

Your thumb muscles tire and your rhythm drifts. It is normal to lose 10 to 25 percent of your opening speed over 30 to 60 seconds.

Can I use the spacebar clicker on a phone?

Yes. Tap the large on-screen key. Touch taps are counted the same way as keyboard presses.

Are my results saved?

Your best score for each time mode and your last 10 runs are stored in your browser only. Clearing your browser data or pressing Clear my scores removes them.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no download and no limit on how many times you can play.
