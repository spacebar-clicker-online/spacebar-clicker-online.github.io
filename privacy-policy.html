Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Spacebar Clicker, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email or any account details to use the tools.
  • Your scores are stored only in your own browser.
  • Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs.
  • We may use Google AdSense to show ads and Google Analytics to understand traffic. These services use cookies.

1. Information you give us

If you contact us through our feedback board on GitHub, we see your GitHub username and whatever you write in your message. These messages are public and are handled under GitHub's own terms and privacy statement. We use them only to reply and to improve the site.

2. Scores stored on your device

Your personal bests, recent runs and settings such as theme and sound are saved using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can delete it with the "Clear my scores" button or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, for security and to keep the service running. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser. Our own pages do not set cookies. Third-party services we use, described below, may set cookies to measure visits and to show and measure ads.

5. Advertising and Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to show advertisements. In connection with this:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info, or in the EU at www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time from the consent message's settings link.

6. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are popular and which devices are used. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information like pages viewed, approximate location and device type. It does not give us your name or contact details. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

7. How information is used

  • To run the website and keep it secure.
  • To understand which tools and guides are useful and improve them.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the site free.
  • To reply to messages you send us through the feedback board.

We do not sell personal information.

8. Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information held about you, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent.

  • EEA and UK (GDPR): where we rely on consent, for example for advertising cookies, you can withdraw it at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To opt out of personalised ads, use the Google links in section 5.

To make a request, contact us through the contact page.

10. External links

Our pages link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, reach us through the contact page.
